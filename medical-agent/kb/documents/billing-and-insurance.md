# Billing and insurance policy

## Statements
Statements are issued monthly. A statement lists the visit date, the amount charged, what the
insurer paid, and the patient balance. Questions about a line item are answered by the billing
office, not by the clinician.

## Insurance changes
Patients must tell the practice when their insurance changes. A visit billed to an inactive
plan is rebilled to the correct plan once the patient provides the policy details.

## Payment plans
A balance can be paid in instalments by agreement with the billing office. A payment plan
does not change the amount owed; it changes the schedule.

## Financial assistance
Patients who cannot pay in full may ask about the practice's financial assistance policy.
Assistance is decided by the billing office and is recorded on the account.

## Disputes
A patient who disagrees with a charge may ask for a review. The practice pauses collection
activity on the disputed amount while the review is open.
