# Privacy and consent policy

## What the practice collects
The practice keeps the information needed to provide care: contact details, visit notes,
results, prescriptions and billing records. It does not collect information that is not
needed for care or payment.

## Who can see a record
Clinical staff see the records they need to treat a patient. Administrative staff see only
the parts needed for scheduling and billing. Every access to a record is logged.

## Sharing with family
A patient may name people who are allowed to receive information. The practice does not
discuss care with anyone else without the patient's consent, except where the law requires
or permits it.

## Portal access
Portal access is personal. Sharing a password is treated as sharing the record itself, and
the practice may suspend access if that happens. A suspected compromise is reported to the
practice immediately.

## Requests and corrections
A patient may ask for a copy of their record or for a correction. The practice answers a copy
request within the period required by law and explains any correction it cannot make.
