# Laboratory results policy

## When results are available
Most laboratory results are available within three business days and are released to the
patient portal when the clinician has reviewed them. Some tests take longer; the portal shows
the request as pending until the result arrives.

## How results are explained
A result is shown with its reference range and a short note. A note is not a diagnosis: the
patient is asked to contact the practice when a result is marked as needing follow-up.

## Asking about a result
Questions about a result are answered by the clinical team. The front desk can confirm that a
result has been received but cannot interpret it.

## Repeat or additional tests
If a result needs repeating, the clinician orders the repeat and the practice contacts the
patient with the reason. A repeat order is not a sign that the first result was wrong.

## Transfer to another practice
A patient may ask for results to be sent to another practice. The request must be in writing
and must name the receiving practice.
