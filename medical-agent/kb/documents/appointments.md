# Appointment scheduling policy

## Booking a visit
Appointments can be booked through the patient portal, by telephone, or at the front desk.
Portal requests are answered within one business day. A booking is confirmed only after the
practice sends a confirmation message; a request alone does not hold a slot.

## Rescheduling and cancelling
A visit can be rescheduled or cancelled up to 24 hours before the start time without a fee.
Later cancellations and missed visits may be recorded as a no-show. Repeated no-shows may
require a deposit for future bookings.

## Urgent same-day visits
Same-day slots are released each morning for urgent problems. They are not used for
routine follow-up or paperwork. A request for a same-day slot is triaged by clinical staff,
who decide whether the patient is seen in person or redirected.

## Waitlist
When a requested slot is full, the front desk offers the waitlist. Being on the waitlist is
not an appointment; the practice contacts patients when a slot opens.

## Late arrival
Patients who arrive more than 15 minutes late may be asked to rebook so that later visits
are not delayed.
