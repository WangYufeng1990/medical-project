# Prescription refill policy

## Eligible refills
Refill requests are accepted through the portal for medicines that already have an active
prescription and remaining refills. Requests are reviewed by the prescribing clinician and
are normally answered within two business days.

## What a refill request must include
A request must name the medicine, the dose, the pharmacy, and the date the last supply runs
out. Requests missing any of these details are returned to the patient instead of being
forwarded to a clinician.

## Medicines that are not refilled this way
Controlled substances, and medicines that need monitoring blood tests, are not refilled from
a request alone. Those patients are offered an appointment so the clinician can review the
treatment.

## Expired prescriptions
An expired prescription cannot be renewed by the practice without a visit. The patient is
offered an appointment, and the request is closed with that explanation.

## If the pharmacy has no record
If the pharmacy cannot find the prescription, the patient is asked to confirm the pharmacy
name and the prescription number before the practice re-sends it.
